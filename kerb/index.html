How it works

Most agencies have thousands of people in the CRM who haven't heard from them in years: appraisals that never listed, buyers who've been in their home five years, past vendors. We get back in touch with them for you.

  1. Step 1

    Send us a CRM export

    A CSV from Rex, VaultRE, AgentBox, Box+Dice or similar. We clean it up, take out duplicates and anyone who's opted out, and split it into appraisals, buyers and vendors.

  2. Step 2

    Approve the messages

    We write 3 or 4 short SMS and emails, sent in your agency's name over about 10 days. Nothing goes out until you've signed off.

  3. Step 3

    Appraisals go in the diary

    We reply to everyone who responds, usually within minutes, and book the interested ones straight into your agent's calendar with notes and a reminder.

What we need from you: a CSV export, access to your agents' calendars, and sign-off on the messages. Usually about an hour of your time.

FAQ

Will this annoy my database?

It's 3 or 4 short messages over about 10 days, offering an updated appraisal to people who've dealt with you before. You approve the wording, and anyone who opts out is removed straight away.

Which CRMs do you work with?

Any CRM that can export a CSV, including Rex, VaultRE, AgentBox, Box+Dice and Eagle. We don't need a login to your CRM.

Do you call people?

No. It's SMS and email only. Replies are drafted with AI so people hear back quickly, and we check the conversations. Your agent does the appraisal as normal.

What if nothing books?

You don't pay anything apart from the SMS costs you approved before launch.

How long does a campaign take?

About a week to set up, then around 10 days of messages. Most bookings come in during the first week of sending.

Why only three founding agencies?

We're working closely with the first agencies to fine-tune the messages. Founding agencies keep the no-setup-fee rate on every future rerun.
