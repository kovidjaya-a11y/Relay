The problem

Thousands of warm contacts, sitting in your CRM.

Every agency has them. Owners who got an appraisal and didn't list. Buyers who bought five years ago and are now outgrowing the house. Vendors who sold, bought again and are ready to move.

They already know your name. Nobody follows them up because your agents are busy chasing today's listings. So those contacts go cold, then list with whoever knocks first.

We follow them up for you, at scale, and you only pay when one turns into an appraisal in your diary.

How it works

From CRM export to a full diary in about 10 days.

  1. 1

    You send us an export

    A CSV from Rex, VaultRE, AgentBox, Box+Dice or any other CRM. We clean it, remove duplicates and existing opt-outs, and split it into old appraisals, past buyers and past vendors.

  2. 2

    You approve every message

    We write a 3–4 message sequence of SMS and email, sent in your agency's name. Nothing goes out until you've signed off on the wording.

  3. 3

    We handle the replies

    AI reads and answers replies within minutes, 7 days a week, and qualifies each owner: timing, address, what's prompting the move. Anything sensitive is handed to a person straight away.

  4. 4

    Appraisals land in your diary

    Confirmed appointments go straight into your agent's calendar with the owner's notes, and the owner gets a reminder. You see every result on a live dashboard.

What counts as a booked appraisal?

A confirmed appointment in your agent's diary, with a named owner at a property address, that the owner confirmed in writing. Enquiries, "maybe later" replies and price questions aren't charged.

No-shows

If the owner doesn't turn up and won't reschedule, tell us within 48 hours and we'll credit it on your next invoice.

Compliance and your data

Built around the Spam Act from day one.

  • Existing relationships only. We message people who have dealt with your agency before, never bought lists.
  • Clearly from you. Every message identifies your agency and how to reach you.
  • Opt-out on every message. Unsubscribes are honoured immediately and handed back to you to update your CRM.
  • Your data stays yours. We use it only to run your campaigns. We never sell, share or reuse it for anyone else.

FAQ

Questions agency principals ask.

Won't this annoy my database?

The sequence is short (3–4 messages over about 10 days), personal and relevant: an updated appraisal on a property they've already asked you about. Anyone who opts out is removed immediately. You approve the tone before anything goes out.

Which CRMs do you work with?

Anything that exports a CSV: Rex, VaultRE, AgentBox, Box+Dice, Eagle and others. We don't need a login to your CRM, just an export and calendar access for booking.

Is it a bot calling my clients?

No. There are no calls and no voice agents. It's text and email only. AI drafts and sends replies so owners hear back in minutes, and a person reviews every conversation. The appraisal itself is done by your agent, as always.

What if the campaign books nothing?

Then you pay nothing apart from the SMS you approved before launch. We only make money when you get appointments, so our incentives match yours.

How much of my time does it take?

About an hour to start: export the CSV, connect the calendar and approve the messages. After that, you and your agents turn up to appraisals.

Why only three founding pilots?

We work closely with each pilot agency to tune the messages and prove the numbers. In return, founding agencies keep the $0 setup rate on every future rerun.
