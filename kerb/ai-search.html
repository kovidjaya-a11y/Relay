FAQ

Is this just SEO?

It overlaps. Good SEO helps, but AI tools also lean heavily on reviews, listings and clear answers on your website. We focus on what makes them name you, and we report on AI recommendations, not just Google rankings.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend us?

No one honestly can, because the AI companies decide. What we can do is fix what's holding you back and show you each month whether you're being named more often.

How long does it take to see a difference?

Some fixes, like your Google profile and listings, can show up within weeks. Website and review changes usually take a few months to have their full effect.

Do I need a new website?

Usually not. Most of the work is adding to what you already have.
