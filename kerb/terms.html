Last updated: 24 September 2026

These terms apply when a real estate agency ("you") engages Kerb Automation (ABN [ADD ABN]) ("we", "us") to run database reactivation campaigns. A signed proposal or written campaign agreement takes priority over these terms where they differ.

1. The service

We clean and segment the contact data you provide, write message sequences for your approval, send SMS and email campaigns in your agency's name, handle replies and book appraisal appointments into the calendar you nominate. We do not make phone calls, give valuations or provide real estate advice.

2. Your responsibilities

  • You confirm that the contacts you give us have an existing relationship with your agency or have otherwise consented to receive messages from you, as required by the Spam Act 2003 (Cth), and that you are allowed to share them with us.
  • You approve every message template before it is sent.
  • You provide calendar access and keep availability up to date, and you tell us about any contacts who must not be messaged.
  • You update your own CRM with the opt-outs we pass back to you.

3. Fees

  • Per booked appraisal: the fee in your proposal (currently $400 plus GST if applicable). A booked appraisal is a confirmed appointment in your nominated calendar with a named owner at a property address, which the owner has confirmed in writing.
  • No-shows: if an owner does not attend and does not reschedule, tell us within 48 hours of the appointment time and we will credit that fee.
  • SMS and messaging costs: passed through at cost with no mark-up, quoted before launch.
  • Setup: $0 for founding pilot agencies.
  • Invoicing: weekly, itemised by appointment, payable within 7 days.

4. No guaranteed results

Response and booking rates depend on the quality and age of your data and on market conditions. Any estimates we give, including those from our website calculator, are illustrations, not promises. Because you pay per booked appraisal, you pay no appraisal fees if none are booked.

5. Data

Your contact data remains yours. We use it only to provide the service to you, handle it in line with our privacy policy, and delete or return it on request once your campaigns end.

6. Ending the service

Either party can stop a campaign at any time by written notice. Fees for appraisals booked before that point remain payable.
