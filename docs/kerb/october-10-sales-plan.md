# October: 10 signed agencies

**What counts as a sale:** a signed pilot agreement plus the CRM export received.
Revenue comes later, as booked appraisals ($400 each).

The list is in `prospects-october.csv` (87 agencies, every state). Open it in
Google Sheets or Excel and work it from the top.

---

## 1. The numbers

| Step | Rate (cold B2B, realistic) | From 87 prospects | Needed for 10 sales |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reach the principal (4 touches) | ~60% | 52 | 170 |
| Get a 30-min call booked | ~20% of reached | 10 | 30 |
| Sign after the call | ~35% | 3–4 | **10** |

**87 prospects gets you about 3–4 sales. 10 sales needs about 170–200
prospects, or a shortcut.** There are three ways to close the gap. Do all
three:

1. **Grow the list to about 200** (1–2 hours). Use the "Find an agency" search on
   ratemyagent.com.au or domain.com.au, one region at a time, and keep only
   independent agencies with 3–15 agents that have been open 5+ years. Or ask me
   for another batch.
2. **Channel partners: one yes can reach many offices.**
   - **One Agency.** Every office is independently owned, it launched 12
     new offices in 2026, and it has new owners as the founders step back
     ([source](https://www.realestatebusiness.com.au/industry/32462-one-agency-reveals-new-owners-as-founders-step-back),
     [source](https://www.realestatebusiness.com.au/industry/32405-one-agency-reaches-12-new-offices-in-2026)).
     Pitch head office on being the recommended database-reactivation
     supplier for its offices, with every office on the same $400-per-appraisal
     terms. New owners are usually looking for member benefits. Nine One Agency
     offices are already on your list, so offer to prove it with 2–3 of them first.
   - **Eview Group.** A network of independent agents that started on the
     Mornington Peninsula ([source](https://au.linkedin.com/company/eviewgroup)).
     Same pitch.
3. **Referrals.** At every signing, ask: "Who's one principal you rate who'd
   want this too?" Principals know each other through REI events and
   co-listing, so a warm intro closes about 3× better than a cold email.

      `agency-outreach.md` §6). Use a free e-sign tool.

## 4. Daily routine (22 business days)

| Time | Do |
|---|---|
| 8:30–9:30 | **Calls.** Principals are in the office before appointments start. Call yesterday's emails. |
| 9:30–10:30 | **10 new first-touch emails** (template in `agency-outreach.md`), each with that agency's pitch link. Connect on LinkedIn at the same time. |
| 12:30–1:00 | Replies and follow-ups. Book calls straight into Calendly. |
| 4:30–5:30 | **Second call block.** Also send Day-8 follow-up emails. |

- **Weekly target:** 50 new prospects touched, 7–8 calls held, 2–3 signed.
- **Avoid:** Saturdays (open homes and auctions), and Monday mornings (sales
  meetings).
- **Track every row** in the Status column: Not contacted → Emailed → Called →
  Call booked → Proposal sent → Signed / Lost (with the reason).

## 5. Don't sign 10 and launch 10 at once

The campaign engine is still semi-manual, and each campaign produces about
30 replies a day in its first week. Stagger the launches: **no more than 2–3
new campaigns a week.** Tell agencies at signing: "Your campaign launches the
week of X." A clear date closes better than "ASAP" anyway.

**Revenue check:** 10 agencies × about 24 appraisals × $400 is about **$96k** in
billings, spread over November–December as the campaigns run. That's the
first 40% of the $236k plan.

## 6. Honest caveats about this list

- Every fact in "Why they fit" comes from the agency's own website or trade
  press (the source link is in the row). Descriptions like "leading" or
  "largest" are their claims, not verified.
- Principal names are included only where the source named them. Everything
  else in the contact columns is for you to fill in.
- A few rows say "check ownership": confirm they're not a corporate-owned
  office before contacting them.
- Your own cold emails to these agencies are commercial messages under the
  Spam Act too. Use a published business address, identify yourself, and
  include an "unsubscribe / reply no" line (the templates already do).
