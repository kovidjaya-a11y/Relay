# Agency outreach playbook: first 3 founding pilots

Goal: 3 signed pilots in 3 weeks. Each prospect gets a personalised pitch link
built from the pitch page:

```
https://kerbautomation.com/pitch.html?agency=Smith%20Realty&name=Jane&contacts=4000&commission=16000
```

(Or open `/pitch.html`, type the numbers into the toolbar and use Print → Save as PDF.)

If you don't know a prospect's contact count, estimate it as **agents × 400**
(a typical agent carries 300–600 past appraisals, buyers and vendors). Use an
average commission of about 2% of the suburb's median price, rounded.

---

## 1. Target list (build 60, expect 3)

**Ideal pilot agency**

- Independent or principal-owned franchise office, 3–15 sales agents
- Established 5+ years, so it has a real database
- Uses Rex, VaultRE, AgentBox or Box+Dice (easy CSV export)
- Suburbs where the median price is $700k or more (bigger commissions, easier ROI)
- Signs of hunger: hiring, a new agent, a new office, "listings wanted" posts,
  a recent drop in active listings

**Where to find them**

- realestate.com.au and Domain agency pages: agent count, sold count, suburbs
- LinkedIn: principal / director / licensee-in-charge
- Local REIx member directories

**Sheet columns:** agency · principal · email · mobile · suburb · agents ·
median price · est. contacts · est. commission · CRM (if known) · pitch link ·
status · next step · date.

Pick one metro region to start (for example, one side of a capital city).
Then the pilots can refer each other, and you can visit in person.

## 2. Sequence (per principal, over about 12 days)

| Day | Channel | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Email | Cold email A with the personalised pitch link |
| 3 | LinkedIn | Connect with a note (one line, no pitch) |
| 5 | Phone | Call the office and ask for the principal (script §4) |
| 8 | Email | Follow-up B (the pre-flight report offer) |
| 12 | Drop-in / SMS | Printed pitch PDF dropped at the office, or a short SMS if they've engaged |

Note: your own cold B2B emails to a published business address are also
commercial electronic messages. Keep your details and an unsubscribe line in
every email.

### Cold email A

> **Subject:** {Agency}'s old appraisals, in listings
>
> Hi {first name},
>
> Quick one. Most agencies have a few thousand past appraisals, buyers and
> vendors that nobody has contacted in years. We reactivate them for you by
> SMS and email in your agency's name, handle every reply, and book
> appraisals straight into your agents' diaries.
>
> I ran rough numbers for {Agency}: about {contacts} contacts is roughly
> {appraisals} booked appraisals and {listings} listings, around {commission}
> in commission. Here's the one-pager: {pitch link}
>
> The catch: there isn't one. $0 setup, and you pay $400 per booked appraisal,
> so nothing if nothing books. I'm taking 3 founding agencies in {region}.
>
> Worth a 15-minute chat this week?
>
> {Your name}, Kerb Automation
> kerbautomation@gmail.com · kerbautomation.com
> Not relevant? Reply "no" and I won't email again.

### Follow-up B

> **Subject:** Free: what's actually in your CRM
>
> Hi {first name}, one more idea. Send me a CSV export (names, mobiles,
> addresses, last contact date) and I'll send back a free pre-flight report
> within 24 hours: how many contacts are reachable, split into old
> appraisals, past vendors and past buyers, plus exactly what a campaign
> would cost you in SMS.
>
> No obligation. The data is used only for the report and deleted afterwards.
> {Your name}

The pre-flight report is the best way into a pilot. Once an agency has seen
"2,140 reachable contacts, 890 old appraisals", the campaign is a small next step.

## 3. Discovery call (15 minutes)

1. "How many appraisals did your team do last year that didn't list?" Let them
   say the number.
2. "Who follows those up now, and how often?" (Usually nobody, or only the top
   agent.)
3. "What's an average commission for you?" Put it into the calculator live
   (share your screen on kerbautomation.com/#calculator).
4. Show the answer: **"$2,000 per new listing, paid only after the appraisal is
   in your diary."**
5. Explain what you need: a CSV export, calendar access for 1–2 agents,
   approval of 4 messages, and about 1 hour of their time.
6. Close: "I can have your pre-flight report back tomorrow and messages going
   out next week. Which agents should the appraisals go to?"

## 4. Phone opener (reception)

> "Hi, it's {name} from Kerb Automation. I emailed {principal} about getting
> their old appraisals back into the diary. Is {principal} around for two
> minutes?"

If they're asked "what's it about?":

> "We book appraisals from the agency's old database, and the agency only pays
> per booked appraisal. I sent a one-pager with their numbers on it."

---

## 5. Objections

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "We already do this / our agents follow up" | "Great. How many of last year's non-listing appraisals got a follow-up in the last 90 days? We only charge on bookings, so if your team has it covered, we'll book very little and you'll pay very little." |
| "Our database will be annoyed" | "Four short messages over 10 days, in your name, which you approve word for word, with opt-out on every one. Anyone who opts out is gone instantly. A typical opt-out rate is a few percent." |
| "AI talking to my clients?" | "It's text only, it never gives prices, and anything sensitive goes straight to a person. You can read every conversation on the dashboard." |
| "$400 is a lot per appraisal" | "It's $2,000 per listing on your own numbers, against {commission} commission. What does a listing cost you on portals or letterbox drops today?" |
| "What about no-shows?" | "Tell us within 48 hours and it's credited." |
| "Is it legal?" | "Your contacts have an existing relationship with you, every message identifies you, and every message has an opt-out. The templates have been checked by our lawyer." (Only say this once it's true.) |
| "Send me some info" | "Sure, the one-pager is at {pitch link}. Can I also run the free pre-flight report on your export? It takes 24 hours and shows exactly what's in there." |

## 6. Pilot agreement checklist (1 page, signed before any data moves)

- Fee: $400 + GST per booked appraisal (the definition from the terms)
- $0 setup (founding pilot); founding rate kept on quarterly reruns
- SMS at cost, with the quote attached
- No-show credit if reported within 48h; cancellations >24h ahead not billed
- Weekly invoice, 7-day terms (direct debit preferred)
- Agency warrants the data qualifies under the Spam Act (existing relationship)
- Agency approves the templates in writing
- Permission to use anonymised results as a case study, plus a testimonial if
  the pilot hits the targets. **Add it to the site as its first real
  result.**
- Either party can stop at any time
