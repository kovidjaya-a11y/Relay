# AI search outreach kit

Service page: kerbautomation.com/ai-search.html
Prospect list: `prospects.csv` (30 local businesses, 24 with email)

The pitch only works if it's specific. Before emailing each business, spend
2 minutes checking what AI actually says about them, then put that in the email.

---

## Step 1: the 2-minute check (do this before every email)

1. Open **ChatGPT** in a temporary chat (top right, so your own history doesn't
   affect the answer).
2. Paste the question from the sheet's "Question to ask AI" column,
   e.g. *Who's the best plumber in Parramatta?*
3. Write down the businesses it names, in order, in the "ChatGPT named" column.
4. Do the same search on **Google** and note who the AI answer at the top names
   (if one shows) in the "Google AI named" column.
5. **Take a screenshot of the ChatGPT answer.** You'll paste it into the email,
   because it's the proof.
6. Mark "Named them?" Y or N:
   - **N** (most will be N) → send **Email A**
   - **Y** → send **Email B**

AI answers vary a bit each time you ask. That's fine; you're reporting what it
said today, honestly.

## When they say yes: the free check report

Send within 2 business days. Keep it to one page in the email body or a Google Doc.

```
Hi [name],

Here's your free AI check for [Business name].

I asked 10 questions your customers ask, across ChatGPT, Google's AI and Perplexity.

| Question | ChatGPT | Google AI | Perplexity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Best [trade] in [suburb]? | Named [Competitor] | Named you | Not named |
| ... | | | |

You were named in [X] of 30 answers. [Top competitor] was named in [Y].

The three biggest reasons:
1. [e.g. Your Google profile has 14 reviews, [Competitor] has 120]
2. [e.g. Your website doesn't say which suburbs you cover]
3. [e.g. You're missing from [directory] where AI finds a lot of its answers]

I can fix all of this for a one-off $500. That covers the full audit, your Google profile and listings, and the website changes, plus a before-and-after report. After that it's optional at $300 a month to keep you there.

Worth a 15-minute call to walk through it? https://calendly.com/kerbautomation/30min

Cheers,
```

What to look at for the "three reasons":
- **Google Business Profile:** review count and rating vs competitors, categories, service areas, photos, whether hours and services are filled in.
- **Website:** does it clearly say what they do, where, and for whom? Are there pages for each service and suburb? Is there a FAQ? Is there business information in the site's code (Schema.org "LocalBusiness" markup)?
- **Listings:** consistent name, address and phone across Google, Yelp, True Local, Yellow Pages, hipages or Oneflare (trades), Healthengine (physios), and law or accounting directories.
- **Reviews mentioning specifics** ("fixed our hot water in Parramatta") help AI connect them to the question.

## Call script (day 2, or straight away for prospects with no email)

> "Hi, it's [name] from Kerb Automation. Quick one: I asked ChatGPT who the best [plumber] in [Parramatta] is, and it recommended [Competitor], not you. I help local businesses fix that. Can I send the owner a free check showing what AI says about you?"

Reception will usually give you the right email. That's the win: send Email A to it straight away.

## Targets

- **Week 1:** check and email all 24 businesses with an email address, and call the 6 without one.
- **Expect:** a much higher reply rate than the real estate emails, because the proof is in the email. Even 3–5 "yes, send it" replies are enough to start.
- **Convert:** free check → $500 audit → $300/month. Two audits in week 2 means $1k, and the first monthly clients.
- **Next niches once one works:** dentists, conveyancers, mechanics, vets, cafes in busy suburbs.
